About

I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, advised by Dr. Doug Bowman. My research explores how people organize information, make sense of complex data, and collaborate in immersive environments, with an interest in how AI can further support these activities.

I design and evaluate XR interaction techniques through controlled experiments and mixed-methods user studies, with the long-term goal of making immersive computing a practical alternative to traditional desktop computing.

Research

Collaborative Spatial Negotiation in XR

I am leading a project in preparation for the IEEE VR 2027 3DUI Contest, designing dual-proposal visualizations, dynamic rubber-band feedback, and perspective switching to help collaborators negotiate shared placements.

Subtle Gaze Guidance in VR

I designed a within-subject eye-tracking study with 18 participants and six cue conditions to investigate how subtle cues redirect attention. I also designed a follow-up study exploring character perception, trust, and narrative understanding in 360° experiences.

Multi-Agent Debiasing with Search-Based Validation

I designed a Judge + Rephrase framework with a search agent to iteratively debias LLM reasoning, evaluated across four models using BBQ-Hard and SQuAD v2. Code on GitHub.
